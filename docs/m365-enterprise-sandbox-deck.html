The Answer

Give coders a safe sandbox —
deploy only what passes

VibOps gives vibe coders a governed place to build and gives platform teams the controls to validate. Build in the sandbox, prove it's safe, then ship. That single move is what makes enterprise adoption possible.

VibOps = Vibe Platform + Vibe Security + Vibe Governance + Vibe Strategy. Vibe Coders build. VibOps ensures they can build safely.
The Distinction

Two different jobs: those who build, and those who make building safe

Before the mechanics, get the vocabulary straight — the rest of this story leans on it.

Vibe Coding
Building applications through natural-language conversation with AI. Users describe what they want; AI writes the code.
Who: business analysts, domain experts, citizen developers
What: apps, automations, integrations
VibOps
The operational discipline of enabling vibe coding safely at enterprise scale — infrastructure, security, governance, and platform engineering.
Who: platform teams, security, IT operations
What: sandboxes, validation, compliance, deployment
The relationship: the CTO decides what and why. VibOps figures out how. Vibe Coders do the building.
