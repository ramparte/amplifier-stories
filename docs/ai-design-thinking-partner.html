The Experiment

One developer designed amplifier-chat by thinking out loud with AI

A developer sat down to design the next generation of Amplifier's interface — a web-based replacement for the CLI, later shipped as amplifier-chat. Instead of sketching alone or scheduling a review, they opened an Amplifier session and started a conversation.

Developer
I'd love to see dynamic UI and leverage the model to figure out the UI for me. Not just build it -- design it.
Amplifier
That's a fascinating direction. Let's explore what "generative UI" means here. If the model decides not just the content but the layout and visual composition...

What followed was not a prompt-response exchange. It was a sustained, 27-turn exploration.

The Ideation Gap

We ask AI to execute — and skip the thinking that matters most

Design in isolation

Product vision starts as a solo exercise -- sketching on whiteboards, drafting docs alone, then scheduling reviews days later for feedback.

AI as executor only

Today's AI workflows excel at "build this component" but rarely join the upstream question: what should we build, and why?

Shallow conversations

Most AI interactions are transactional -- a prompt, a response, done. The deep exploration that produces breakthroughs is rare.

The Shift

The unlock: AI as a thinking partner, not just an executor

How It Unfolded

The session didn't answer a prompt — it sustained an exploration

Opening
Generative UI exploration
What if the AI decides not just content but layout, rendering, and visual composition? Interfaces that reshape themselves to the context.
Middle exploration
Conversation-centered design
Layouts where conversation is the primary interface -- faded history with scroll-back, collapsible tool outputs, progressive disclosure.
Convergence
Philosophy crystallization
From exploration to conviction. The session produced the principle: "we worry about the experience, not the token."
The Differentiator

Research agents enriched the brainstorm while ideas were still forming

While the creative conversation flowed, Amplifier dispatched research agents in parallel — pulling in context faster than any one person could gather it, during the brainstorm rather than before or after.

Competitive analysis

Surveyed existing AI chat interfaces, identifying patterns and gaps in the market.

UX pattern research

Retrieved conversation UI patterns, progressive-disclosure approaches, and information-density studies.

User mental models

Analyzed how developers think about chat vs. IDE vs. terminal interfaces.

Like a research assistant fetching references while you sketch on the whiteboard.

The Takeaway

Start with a question,
not a task.

The most valuable moment in any project is the beginning, where assumptions form and direction is set. Bring AI there — not to build, but to think with you.

AI moves upstream

If AI can participate where direction is set, everything downstream improves -- not just faster execution, but better decisions about what to execute.

Depth over speed

The value wasn't a quick answer. It was the late insight the opening question couldn't reach -- discovered through exploration, not decreed from above.
