The Turn

Nobody told Amplifier how. Sam just said “organize my tabs.”

Amplifier worked out on its own that it could drive AppleScript — macOS’s built-in app-automation bridge — through the terminal to read Microsoft Edge. No plugin. No integration. No configuration. It just figured it out.

That self-directed discovery — not the tab count — is the real result.
What AppleScript Is

macOS quietly lets apps expose their own controls — and Edge exposes every tab

AppleScript is a way for programs to talk to other programs. Each app chooses what to expose: window titles, tab URLs, document contents. Microsoft Edge exposes everything about your tabs — and Amplifier has access to the terminal.

Put those two facts together and an AI can read every tab title, URL, and window in seconds — without a single line of custom integration code.
