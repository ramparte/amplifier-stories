The Category Trap

Ask a generic AI to design, and you get everyone else’s dashboard

Most developers lack formal design training. When they hand design work to an AI assistant, the result is category-matched output—a healthcare dashboard that looks like every other healthcare dashboard.

Without deep knowledge of color theory, typography systems, motion, and spatial composition, AI-generated designs hit a ceiling: competent, but uninspired. The fix isn’t more prompting—it’s expertise.

Same request, two outcomes
Without Design Intelligence
“Design a dashboard” → Research dashboards → Competent remix of existing UIs
With Design Intelligence
“Design a dashboard” → Decompose cognitive tasks → Fresh work grounded in proven patterns

The Cast

Seven specialists, each owning one slice of the craft

Before we show how the quality is earned, meet the agents that collaborate on every design:

Build the system
  • design-system-architect — system-wide tokens, foundations, architecture
  • component-designer — individual components, props, and variants
  • layout-architect — page layout, information architecture, grids
  • responsive-strategist — responsive strategy and breakpoint design
Give it a soul
  • art-director — aesthetic strategy and visual coherence
  • animation-choreographer — motion sequences, easing, choreography
  • voice-strategist — tone, messaging, and content strategy
